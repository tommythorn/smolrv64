# SmolRV64 -- Geekbench 5.4.1 (RISC-V preview), 2026-11-01, the aligned frontend with a 64-halfword ring

Board build of a814eba8 (github main b9eeb429): the frontend is 7f8f5a94's again -- the I$ answers one
16-byte-aligned pair a request and the predictor reads one 2K-entry skewed BTB in its loop -- with the fetch
ring at 64 halfwords, so a stream that is not 16-byte aligned fetches as an aligned one, and three
cycle-identical timing cuts (the DDR port's ready replicated beside its loads, the ring's head checked a cycle
late, fetch's outcomes selected by the bundle's last slot). WNS +0.002 ns (`ExtraNetDelay_high`), gate PASS on
the 4 GiB board (login, zero faults) and a `cargo check` of Simmerv on it (47 min 53 s); a fresh boot, Ubuntu
over NFS, Geekbench run alone under `perf stat` (the `td` set, every counter at 100%) with nothing else on the
board (one ssh to start it, the results read over NFS); no faults.
