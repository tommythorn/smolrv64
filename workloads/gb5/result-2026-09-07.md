own port and a second ALU, the run-ahead fetch buffer with its page-crossing fix, the T1

Machine Learning stands out: 0.01 images/sec, the same as the June baseline, while every
other subtest moved 4-10x. Its own stack (the traced run of the same day, single-core
window, `tools/gb5-trace-stack.py`): 28.3 G instructions in 78.5 G cycles, IPC 0.36, traps
0.04 per thousand instructions, LSU 61%, FPU 21%, D$ miss 4.4%. No trap storm and no slow
path: the core runs it at a normal IPC, and the rate is the instruction count -- ~6 G
scalar instructions per image against the reference machine's SIMD. Gaussian Blur (IPC
0.40) and Structure from Motion (0.43) read the same way. The ISA (no vector extension)
sets those three scores; the core's only lever on them is the D$ miss share.
